# Ethics

wwxd is for learning from what people have said and written in public. It turns
their talks, podcasts and essays into a wiki where every quote points back to its
source. Use it in that spirit.

## Rules

- **Cite your sources.** Every answer must show where the person said what it
  claims. When the sources don't cover a question, the answer says so, and any
  extrapolation is marked as the agent's, not the person's. Keep the citations
  when you share an answer.
- **Don't impersonate.** A vault answer is a cited summary of someone's public
  positions. Never present it as the real person speaking, endorsing something or
  giving advice. Don't use a vault to write posts, messages or reviews in their
  name.
- **Public people, public material.** Build vaults of people who publish their
  thinking, from material they chose to make public. Don't build vaults of private
  individuals, such as a coworker, a former partner or a neighbor. Don't add
  leaked recordings, private messages or paywalled content you haven't paid for.
- **Respect platform terms and copyright.** wwxd fetches public captions, articles
  and podcast audio for your own use. You are responsible for following the terms
  of the sites you fetch from and for respecting copyright. This repo ships source
  configs, never content.
- **Don't publish what you can't share.** A vault holds full transcripts and long
  quotes. Keep it private unless you have the right to share that material.

## Opt-out

The example recipes in `src/wwxd/examples/` name real people. If you are one of
them, or represent one of them, and want your recipe removed,
[open an issue](https://github.com/MSliwa01/wwxd/issues/new) with the recipe name.
We remove the recipe and don't add it back. You don't need to give a reason.

Removing a recipe doesn't delete vaults that people already built on their own
machines, because wwxd keeps no central copy.
